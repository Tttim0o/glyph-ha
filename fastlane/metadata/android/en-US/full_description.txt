Glyphs drives the Nothing Phone (1) glyph interface and the camera flash directly through the kernel LED driver.

Features
- Manual control of every glyph zone, with brightness sliders and presets.
- Animation composer: build step sequences, preview them on screen, then play them on the glyphs or save them.
- Shake twice to toggle the torch; choose glyphs, flash, or both.
- Charging-tap: tap the back twice while charging to show the battery level on the bottom line.
- Record your own phone motion and bind it to a glyph or flash effect.
- Unread-notification glow and music-synced bar when the phone is face-down with the screen off.
- Home Assistant integration through a token-protected broadcast intent API.

Requirements
- Nothing Phone (1), rooted with Magisk or KernelSU (root is required to write the LED sysfs nodes).
- Android 10 or newer.
- Optional: notification access (for unread counts).

Privacy
No network access, no analytics, no accounts. Gesture data stays on the device.
